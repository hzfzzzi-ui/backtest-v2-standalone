## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Negative Conditional Context Factor Catalog
System MUST expose a research-only catalog for the mainline negative conditional context family, including factor metadata, direction, frequency, and as-of policy.

### Requirement: Negative Conditional Context Snapshots
System MUST generate daily symbol-level snapshots for the negative conditional context family from the real mainline breakout runtime context.

### Requirement: Negative Conditional Context Study Surface
System MUST support a study surface that evaluates the family as fragility/context factors under the 2012-anchored three-window contract.
