## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Execution Pressure Factor Catalog
System MUST expose a research-only catalog for stock sleeve execution pressure factors derived from `portfolio_daily.csv`.

### Requirement: Execution Pressure Snapshots
System MUST generate daily account-level snapshots for execution pressure factors, including raw/normalized values and as-of-safe traces.

### Requirement: Execution Pressure Study Surface
System MUST support a study surface that evaluates execution pressure against future stock sleeve returns under the 2012-anchored three-window contract.
